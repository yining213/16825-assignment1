1. Practicing with Cameras 15 points

1.1 360-degree Renders 5 pointsdone

A gif showing the cow mesh from many continuously changing viewpoints, by constructing a set of camera extrinsics around the mesh and rendering one frame per azimuth.

Approach

The camera is placed on a circle around the origin with look_at_view_transform(dist=3, elev=0, azim=azim) and a FoVPerspectiveCameras with a 60° field of view. The mesh is built once and reused across frames; each rendered frame is clipped to [0, 1], converted to uint8, and the stack is written out with imageio.mimsave.
